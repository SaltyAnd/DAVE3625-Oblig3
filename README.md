# Blueflow

Blueflow is a simple fluid-tracking app for keeping an eye on daily water and other drink intake.

## Features

- **Track water and other drinks separately.** Log water and alternative fluids such as coffee, milk, soda, or juice.
- **See your intake at a glance.** The dashboard displays water, alternative fluid, and combined totals. A color-coded progress bar compares intake with your daily water goal.
- **Set a personal water goal.** Change the daily goal; the progress percentage and remaining amount update automatically. The goal starts at 2,000 mL as an editable example, not a medical recommendation.
- **Log common and custom amounts.** Quick-add a 250 mL glass, a 500 mL medium bottle, or a 1 L large bottle. Enter a different amount or create named custom containers for quick reuse.
- **Use metric units your way.** Switch between millilitres and litres. Amounts are stored in millilitres, so changing the display unit does not change your logs.
- **Review today’s drinks.** The list shows each drink’s name, amount, and time. Remove an entry if you logged it by mistake. Only today’s drinks can be added or removed.
- **Check past daily totals.** Browse the calendar to see water, alternative-fluid, and combined totals for a selected date. Past individual drink lists are not shown.
- **Keep your data in your browser.** Entries, custom containers, goal, and unit preference are saved locally on your device.
